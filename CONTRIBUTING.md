# Contributing

PowerFlex is an early-stage research prototype. Contributions that improve reproducibility, correctness, privacy, or evaluation quality are welcome.

Before opening a pull request:

- Describe the behavior or evidence that changed and how to reproduce it.
- Keep claims aligned with the data and limitations documented in the project.
- Do not include private scheduler snapshots, job identifiers, secrets, or company telemetry.
- Preserve source attribution and the full NLR data-use notice when modifying or redistributing NLR-derived data.
- Explain any changes to the scheduling objective, hard constraints, units, time zones, or assumptions.
